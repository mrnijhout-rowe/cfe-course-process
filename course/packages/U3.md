# U3 packages: Unit 3, for spring 2027

Same shape as the Unit 4 packages, from the outline's Unit 3 section.
Formats: course/formats/. Built before Unit 4 is taught, from what
fall 2026 taught of chapters 5 and 6 and the FEEDBACK.md entries on it
and on the text adventure; REV-3, at the end of this file, folds in
what fall teaches later (Dan, 2026-10-08). U3-PLAN starts once
UNIT_2_PLAN.md is approved, because these lessons may use only what
its "New in this lesson" table introduced.

- **U2-PLAN and U3-PLAN** each carry what
  course/formats/UNIT_AND_PROJECT.md asks of a unit plan, in the shape
  of UNIT_4_PLAN.md less its comparison table and its "Fall 2026
  lines," and list the departures from book order with their reasons.
  Each also writes the unit's Canvas page (`PAGE_2_Unit.md`,
  `PAGE_3_Unit.md`), as `PAGE_4_Unit.md` does for Unit 4.
- **U3-A** (3.1 to 3.5): reading quizzes for chapter 5 through
  "Nested Conditionals" and chapter 6 through "Boolean functions."
  Codio's U8.L4 (at 3.4) and U8.L2 (at 3.5) both use a list on two
  pages; each plan says so in one line.
- **U3-B** (3.6 to 3.9): one reading quiz on the recursion sections
  of chapters 5 and 6; `CHECK_3.6`, the learning check on lessons 3.1
  to 3.5. Lessons 3.6 and 3.7 have no chapter behind them, so their
  plans carry a little more explanation for Dan. Lesson 3.6 teaches
  the five habits from the text adventure's lesson on handling what
  the player types, with a `while` loop doing the asking again.
- **U3-C** (3.10 to 3.13): `PROJECT_3_Text_Game.md` with the three
  options, one brief per option, and its Canvas page
  `PROJECT_3_Page.md`.
  sources/fall_2026_text_based_adventure/ is the starting point: same
  stages, same times, same rubric shape. Two things change because of
  where the project now sits: `.strip().lower()` and asking again are
  already taught in 3.6, so the short lesson inside the project
  becomes a reminder, and a scene function that calls itself can be
  called recursion.
- **U3-Q**: `QUIZ_3_Unit_Quiz.md` and `CODIO_3_Quiz_Review/`. It asks
  about the project the way the fall 2026 quiz asked about the text
  adventure. About a fifth of the points come from Units 1 and 2.
- **REV-3**: edits to the Unit 3 files from the FEEDBACK.md entries
  fall 2026 adds while Unit 4 and the speed run are taught in this
  build's lesson shape, and from the fall recursion taster if Dan runs
  one. It runs after the fall semester ends and before spring's first
  coding lesson, the way REV-4 does for Unit 4.
