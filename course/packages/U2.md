# U2 packages: Unit 2, for spring 2027

Same shape as the Unit 4 packages, from the outline's Unit 2 section.
Formats: course/formats/. Built before Unit 4 is taught, from what
fall 2026 taught of chapters 1 to 4 and the FEEDBACK.md entries on it;
REV-2, at the end of this file, folds in what fall teaches later (Dan,
2026-10-08). units/02-python-foundations/UNIT_2_AS_TAUGHT.md is the
record of fall's version of this content.

- **U2-PLAN and U3-PLAN** each carry what
  course/formats/UNIT_AND_PROJECT.md asks of a unit plan, in the shape
  of UNIT_4_PLAN.md less its comparison table and its "Fall 2026
  lines," and list the departures from book order with their reasons.
  Each also writes the unit's Canvas page (`PAGE_2_Unit.md`,
  `PAGE_3_Unit.md`), as `PAGE_4_Unit.md` does for Unit 4.
- **U2-A** (2.1 to 2.4): reading quizzes for chapters 1 and 2. Lesson
  2.1 includes getting Thonny installed and a first file saved.
- **U2-B** (2.5 to 2.10): reading quizzes for chapters 3 and 4;
  `CHECK_2.6`, the learning check on lessons 2.1 to 2.5;
  `MINI_2.10_Turtle_Art.md`, which counts as a small project grade.
  Turtle code is run in a window if the session can open one.
- **U2-Q**: `QUIZ_2_Unit_Quiz.md` and `CODIO_2_Quiz_Review/`. The fall
  2026 quiz and review guide are the starting point, because most of
  their questions are on this unit already. The text adventure
  questions come out; turtle and `input()` questions go in. About a
  fifth of the points stay on Unit 1.
- **REV-2**: edits to the Unit 2 files from the FEEDBACK.md entries
  fall 2026 adds while Unit 4 and the speed run are taught in this
  build's lesson shape: how the period ran, peer instruction, try-its
  and partner challenges, learning checks, and the project stages. It
  runs after the fall semester ends and before spring's first coding
  lesson, the way REV-4 does for Unit 4.
