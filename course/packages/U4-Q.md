five reading quizzes, the project spec, and
sources/assessments/unit_2_quiz/ (the quiz file, every page of
Codio_Review, and two or three of the JSON files).

`materials/CODIO_4_Quiz_Review/`, in the formats in
course/formats/ASSESSMENTS.md.

  stages, reading a file, choosing between a list and a dictionary.

  dictionaries and tuples; the project. Its pages say "data
  structures," not "Unit 4."
