last updated 1/21/2026). Course runs fall 2026 (two sections) and spring
2027 (one section). Four meetings per week: three 50-minute and one
90-minute, on a rotating timetable, so weekdays below are calendar
days, not meeting days.

Meeting budget after Fall Break: weeks 8 through 14 at four meetings
each, less one for the short week 11, is about 27 meetings, plus
possibly one on Dec 7. In 50-minute-lesson terms that is roughly 34
lesson slots. With ~12 meetings reserved for the speed run and final
project, about 15 to 16 meetings (18 to 20 lesson slots) remain for
content, which is one unit of three and a half to four weeks.
